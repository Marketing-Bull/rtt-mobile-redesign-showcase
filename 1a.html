Questions, answered

What gifts should I avoid?▾

Avoid flowers (infection risk), strong scents (nausea triggers) and sugary foods. Every item here is safe and doctor-recommended.

What are the most common side effects of chemo?▾

Nausea, dry mouth, fatigue, skin sensitivity, hair loss and "chemo brain." This package addresses each.

Can I make dietary substitutions?▾

Yes — sugar-free, vegan, dairy-free, nut-free, gluten-free, kosher and organic. Note your needs at checkout.
